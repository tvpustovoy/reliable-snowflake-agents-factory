Digital employees on the ODE standard, built on Snowflake

We took the open Open Digital Employee standard we have been working on and, building on it, create digital employees for small businesses on Snowflake (pilot: RecPlace). The goal is not just to build an agent. It is a digital employee you can delegate to: it acts proactively under uncertainty and is accountable for results. ODE, TARA and OHC are openly published approaches and methods, not code. Everything on Snowflake is built during the hackathon.

Our implementation methods (building blocks)

01Knowledge distillation

How to prepare the company environment (condition 03) from a large body of raw data, automatically and with evidence.
Why the standard needs this method
ODE requires trusted, fresh sources but does not say how to get them out of 13.8M tokens of chats and CRM. A single long context yields a retelling, not facts you can check. Distillation runs in waves over a shared field of facts; facts are checked against each other, and contradictions and unknowns are flagged as the work goes.
What it gives
  • every fact is a verbatim quote checked by SQL;
  • independent confirmation from different people;
  • incremental and scalable: time depends on the number of waves, not on corpus size.

02Self-evolving organization

How the company environment is structured, how an employee is compiled from it, and how it improves under control (conditions 03, 05).
Why the standard needs this method
ODE describes package layers and requires controlled improvement. We provide the structure of the world model: 5 Books, each checked in its own way, a conflict engine and a codex. We also provide compilation of the employee from it and the loop observe → propose → ratify → enact.
What it gives
  • one world model → many employees (COMPILE_AGENT);
  • each employee carries only its own projection;
  • v → v+1 only through the owner, with no silent mandate change.

03TARA — acting under uncertainty

How the employee acts proactively when knowledge is contradictory and incomplete (conditions 02, 04).
Why the standard needs this method
ODE starts from an imperfect world: data is missing and interests conflict. It does not give decision rules, though. TARA distinguishes knowledge states and forbids treating missing data as zero, consent or permission. It also defines a decision test.
What it gives
  • ACT / HYPOTHESIS / ASK / APPROVE: decision rights and escalation;
  • a map of what is unknown, by domain;
  • a next step: who, signal, loss limit, review date.
In Snowflake

05Open Human Context

How to keep the evidence honest and respect the data boundaries of people (conditions 03, 04; Alignment pillar).
Why the standard needs this method
ODE requires respecting privacy, consent and data boundaries, and a company is its people: the founder alone accounts for 62% of internal utterances. OHC keeps experience separate from interpretation and gives each record 6 status axes. It holds a conflict open until its owner decides and releases only a projection under a grant.
What it gives
  • a new interpretation never overwrites the fact;
  • the history of knowledge is a query, so nothing is lost;
  • conclusions about people are hypotheses, not ratings; access is logged.

What the ODE standard gives

A definition of “ready to delegate”

Five conditions: measurable outcomes, domain expertise, a prepared working environment, governance and accountability, and controlled improvement. Together they tell us what must be true before a business can hand work to a digital employee.

A package to build

Three layers: domain expertise, company environment and the employee itself. The hardest layer is the company environment, which is usually prepared by hand. We build it automatically from raw data.

Backward design

Start from the outcome (baseline, target, owner), then form a work hypothesis and say what would disprove it, then build, pilot and measure. Finally keep, change or stop.

A way to prove trust

Performance · Reliability · Alignment × 5 areas, with no single score: a strength in one place does not cancel a failure in another. This becomes our trust dashboard.
