# Research — concepts behind Firn

Approaches and methods we use to build digital employees on Snowflake. ODE and TARA are open standards and approaches (not code) that we develop and publish; knowledge distillation and the self-evolving organization are our way of implementing ODE on Snowflake. All implementation code is written during the hackathon.

**Start here:** [00-concepts-overview.html](00-concepts-overview.html) — why each concept is needed and what it gives.

| # | Concept | Role in the project |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | [Knowledge distillation](01-hive-distillation.html) ([md](01-hive-distillation.md)) | builds the company environment (ODE condition 03) from raw data, provably and at scale |
| 02 | [Self-evolving organization](02-self-evolving-organization.html) ([md](02-self-evolving-organization.md)) | world model structure (5 Books, engine, codex), compiling employees, controlled evolution |
| 03 | [TARA — acting under uncertainty](03-tara-acting-under-uncertainty.html) ([md](03-tara-acting-under-uncertainty.md)) | ACT / HYPOTHESIS / ASK / APPROVE, knowledge states, next-step contract |
| 04 | [Open Digital Employee](04-open-digital-employee.html) ([md](04-open-digital-employee.md)) | the standard we implement: 5 conditions, 3 layers, 3 pillars × 5 areas |
