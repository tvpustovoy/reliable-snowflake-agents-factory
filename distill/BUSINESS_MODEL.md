# Business model structure (what we distill into)

This is the **skeleton of the company model** Firn builds. Every fact is placed into exactly one `zone` and one `subzone`. The coverage map (`MODEL.V_COVERAGE`) is computed over this structure, so judges and the founder see *how much of the company we know, where it conflicts, and where we are blind*. New agents are compiled from slices of this structure.

Machine-readable copy: `sql/00_seed_model.sql` → table `RECPLACE.MODEL.ZONES` (generated by `sql/gen_seed.py`). **Edit this file, then re-run the generator.**

Company: RecPlace — B2B SaaS, geomarketing for small businesses (Russia). Founder-led sales team, CRM, calls, chats, product wiki.

**Cross-cutting fact types** (any zone): `promise`, `risk`, `decision`, `rule`; explicit unknowns go to signals (`gap`, `question`).

**Coverage rule.** A subzone is *covered* when it has ≥ 5 accepted facts from ≥ 2 independent carriers; *thin* with 1–4; *blind* with 0. Blind subzones are shown, never hidden, and become the next wave's need.
